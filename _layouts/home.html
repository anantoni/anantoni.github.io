Research

Academic research

PhD student in static program analysis at the Department of Informatics and Telecommunications, University of Athens, advised by Yannis Smaragdakis (PLaST group). I work on precise, scalable analysis of Java programs: pointer analysis, enterprise applications and TensorFlow shapes. Earlier: research intern at Oracle Labs Australia and technical student at CERN.

Publications

Applied research at Lore Armory

I founded Lore Armory to publish exact, verified game-mechanics research with open data and open-source tools. The Palworld stat-formula paper (v3.1) comes with a dataset, a free calculator and an open-source save reader.
